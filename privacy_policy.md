# Privacy Policy for Legal Lens

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

**Legal Lens** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web services collect, use, disclosure, and safeguard your information when you use our application **Legal Lens** (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
* **User Accounts:** Name, email address, password, and user role (Consumer / Enforcement Officer) when creating an account or logging in.
* **Scan & Query History:** Products searched, barcoded scans, and OCR label text submitted for compliance evaluation.

### B. Device Permissions & Camera Access
* **Camera Access:** The App requests access to your device camera solely for scanning product barcodes (EAN/UPC) and capturing product label text for OCR compliance verification. Camera streams are processed locally or transmitted securely to our AI compliance engine for analysis.
* **Network / Internet Access:** Used to communicate with backend APIs to fetch product compliance databases, FSSAI regulatory standards, and verified product reports.

### C. Automatically Collected Technical Data
* Device model, operating system version, app version, and standard network diagnostics to ensure app performance and security.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following core purposes:
1. **FSSAI Compliance Verification:** Analyzing product labels and barcodes against Indian Legal Metrology Rules and FSSAI standards.
2. **Consumer Empowerment:** Providing product scorecards, ingredient analysis, health flags, and violation reporting tools.
3. **Officer Dashboards:** Facilitating regulatory inspections, complaint tracking, and non-compliance verification.
4. **App Optimization:** Debugging errors, improving AI label parsing accuracy, and ensuring platform security.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

* **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted between the App and our backend servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.
* **Data Retention:** Scan history and user preferences are retained securely to allow users to access their past compliance reports.
* **No Selling of Data:** We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal information, scan logs, or usage history to third-party advertisers.
